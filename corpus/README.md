# Corpus

The corpus will contain synthetic, license-clean fixtures frozen before implementation.

Private product evidence may be replayed through a local adapter, but private or internal content must not be copied into this public-shaped corpus.

Each fixture must declare:

- candidate artifact;
- execution evidence;
- catalog snapshot;
- owner policy;
- expected disposition;
- expected required and forbidden reason codes;
- why the fixture exists;
- provenance classification.
